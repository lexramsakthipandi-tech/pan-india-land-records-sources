## 3. Central Statutory Registries & Court Portals

1. **CERSAI (Central Registry of Securitisation Asset Reconstruction & Security Interest of India)**
   - Official Portal: https://cersai.org.in
   - Usage: Mandatory asset search by survey number, plot number, or borrower PAN to verify existing bank mortgages nationwide under SARFAESI.

2. **e-Courts National Portal (Department of Justice)**
   - Official Search Engine: https://services.ecourts.gov.in
   - Usage: CNR number, case number, or litigant search to uncover pending civil suits, partition suits, and interim injunctions (Lis Pendens).

3. **MCA-21 (Ministry of Corporate Affairs)**
   - Official Portal: https://www.mca.gov.in
   - Usage: View Index of Charges for companies (Form CHG-1 mortgage filings, Form CHG-4 satisfaction of charges).

4. **State RERA National Master Portals**
   - Maharashtra: https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in
   - Karnataka: https://rera.karnataka.gov.in
   - Tamil Nadu: https://rera.tn.gov.in
   - Uttar Pradesh: https://up-rera.in
   - Haryana: https://haryanarera.gov.in

## 4. Leading Supreme Court Benchmark Precedents (With Official Citations)

1. **Suraj Lamp & Industries Pvt. Ltd. v. State of Haryana**
   - Official Citations: *(2012) 1 SCC 656 / AIR 2012 SC 206*
   - Ratio: Transfer of property by GPA, Agreement to Sell, and Will (SA-GPA-WILL) conveys no title. Title passes only by registered conveyance deed under Section 54 TPA.

2. **Vineeta Sharma v. Rakesh Sharma & Ors.**
   - Official Citations: *(2020) 9 SCC 1 / 2020 INSC 483*
   - Ratio: Daughters have equal coparcenary rights by birth under Section 6 of the Hindu Succession Act; father need not have been alive on 09.09.2005. Partitions registered prior to 20.12.2004 are saved.

3. **Shakeel Ahmed v. Syed Akhlaq Hussain**
   - Official Citations: *2023 INSC 965 / (2024) 12 SCC 350*
   - Ratio: Reaffirmed Suraj Lamp; an unregistered Agreement to Sell cannot confer title or support a suit for possession under Section 54 TPA and Section 17 Registration Act.

4. **Nahalchand Laloochand Pvt. Ltd. v. Panchali Co-op Hsg. Soc.**
   - Official Citations: *(2010) 9 SCC 536 / AIR 2010 SC 3607*
   - Ratio: Promoter has no right to sell stilt or open car parking spaces as independent units. They are common areas and facilities that vest in the housing society.

5. **Celir LLP v. Bafna Motors (Mumbai) Pvt. Ltd. & Ors.**
   - Official Citations: *(2024) 2 SCC 1 / 2023 INSC 847*
   - Ratio: Borrower's right of redemption under Section 13(8) SARFAESI Act extinguishes on publication of the public auction notice.

6. **Kale & Ors. v. Deputy Director of Consolidation**
   - Official Citations: *(1976) 3 SCC 119 / AIR 1976 SC 807*
   - Ratio: An oral family settlement followed by a memorandum recording a past partition does not require registration; if it creates rights in praesenti, registration is compulsory under Section 17.

7. **Manchegowda & Ors. v. State of Karnataka**
   - Official Citations: *(1984) 3 SCC 301 / AIR 1984 SC 1151*
   - Ratio: Constitutional validity of PTCL Act upheld. Alienation of SC/ST granted land without prior government permission is null and void ab initio; land is subject to summary resumption without compensation.

8. **Jagpal Singh & Ors. v. State of Punjab**
   - Official Citations: *(2011) 11 SCC 396 / AIR 2011 SC 1123*
   - Ratio: Village common lands (*Gomala, Gairan, Gram Sabha*, catchment areas) cannot be regularized or alienated to private encroachers; mandatory eviction ordered nationwide.

9. **In Re: Interplay between Arbitration Agreements and Indian Stamp Act**
   - Official Citations: *2023 INSC 1066 (7-Judge Constitution Bench)*
   - Ratio: Non-stamping or under-stamping is a curable defect under the Stamp Act and does not render the underlying contract void ab initio.

## 5. Institutional Bank Scrutiny Manuals & Regulatory Standards

   - 30-year root-of-title tracing mandate
   - Form 15 Encumbrance Certificate continuity requirement

2. **HDFC Bank & ICICI Bank Conveyancing Standards**
   - Guidelines on Tripartite Builder Agreements, Supplementary Allocation Agreements, and Society NOCs
   - CERSAI search verification protocols

3. **Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Master Directions**
   - Master Direction - Priority Sector Lending (Housing Loans) & Master Circular on Housing Finance
   - Mandatory registration of equitable mortgages on CERSAI portal within 30 days of disbursement.
